The SOW POW framework represents stages of financial maturity and intentional wealth building — a path you can walk with clarity at every season of life.

The SOW POW Framework

Seven stages of intentional wealth — from preparing the ground to writing what endures.

Stage 0 🌱

Prepare The Ground

Cultivate the mindset and clarity required before wealth can take root in your life.

Stage 1 🪨

Secure The Soil

Establish reserves, reduce fragile debt, and create the stability every future layer depends on.

Stage 2 🏛

Own The Structure

Build legal and tax architecture that protects what you earn and how you grow.

Stage 3 💧

Water The Roots

Develop liquidity systems and instruments that nourish growth without uprooting your foundation.

Stage 4 🛡

Protect The Harvest

Shield retirement income and long-term gains with strategies designed to outlast market seasons.

Stage 5 🚪

Open The Gates

Extend your planning horizon and create intentional access to opportunity for yourself and others.

Stage 6 📜

Write The Legacy

Formalize the story, structures, and instructions that continue growing after you are gone.
